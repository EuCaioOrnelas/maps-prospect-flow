- Wiize Pay nas vendas: venda criada depois de integration_connections.connected_at é cobrada pelo Wiize Pay (lead_deals.billing_provider='wiize_pay'); anteriores ficam 'Controle interno' — por que: preserva o histórico e evita cobrar vendas antigas.
- Cadastro com Wiize Pay conectado usa uma única tela e salva a venda antes da cobrança externa, sem rollback automático — por que: falha da cobrança não pode apagar o fato comercial nem seus anexos.
- Clientes do CRM vão ao Wiize Pay por fila (wiize_pay_customer_sync_queue) acordada por gatilho no banco, lotes de 100 e reenvio com espera crescente — por que: edições vêm de muitos lugares do sistema e falhas não podem perder clientes.

- Chamadas à API do Wiize Pay tiram um `/v1` final de `WIIZE_PAY_API_BASE_URL` e usam `<base>/v1/<recurso>` (customers, charges) — por que: o secret atual já termina em `/api/public/v1`, e somar `/v1` de novo dá 404.
- `DialogContent` aceita `overlayClassName` para exceções visuais pontuais sem alterar o fundo de todos os modais — por que: mantém o padrão global estável e permite campanhas específicas.
- WiizePay embeds use a borderless, height-reserved viewport with internal scrolling, loading feedback, and message origin/source validation against the approved ticket URL — why: avoids nested framing, layout shifts, artificial delays, and untrusted cross-window messages.
- WiizePay entry promotions keep rotation history per authenticated user in browser storage and record impressions only after the connection check succeeds — why: account changes and connected users must not consume or share the sequence.
- Dark theme colors use relative HSL from the reference OKLCH palette in global CSS; special surfaces consume semantic tokens — why: preserves source color fidelity and opacity utilities without altering the light theme.
- Public marketing SectionHeading renders its eyebrow only with explicit showEyebrow opt-in — why: avoids redundant heading decoration while permitting necessary context.
- Auth account synchronization is single-flight and throttled; repeated SIGNED_IN for the same user is not a new login — why: tab refocus must not duplicate account reads or disrupt page state.
- Chat startup reads and second-factor status reads have bounded waits with retry; second-factor errors never unlock protected content — why: transient failures must neither leave an endless spinner nor bypass verification.
- Every Stripe plan/add-on price ID must be mapped in stripe-webhook, check-subscription, get-stripe-mrr and audit-mrr (create-stripe-trial only creates) — why: an unmapped price turns paying customers into "free" on the first charge.
- Card-trial signup completes the profile (plan, terms acceptance, trial dates from Stripe, CPF, address, card) through create-stripe-trial action complete_profile with service role and cancels the Stripe subscription if account creation fails — why: before email confirmation the user has no session, the production signup trigger may lag behind, and no one may be charged without an account.
- Stripe cancellation webhooks never downgrade a profile while its running trial uses another subscription or the customer still has another live subscription — why: signup retries cancel orphan subscriptions and those events arrive after the new account exists.
- Projects module (projects, project_stages, project_tasks, checklist, activity, files) syncs deadlines into the existing Agenda via DB triggers using calendar_events.project_id/project_task_id with unique indexes; progress counters and activity are written only by triggers — why: one idempotent source of truth, no duplicate events, no client-side recalculation.
- Agenda all-day events (project deadlines) are excluded from the overlap constraint and from the Google push — why: due dates must not block meeting slots or be pushed as 24h meetings.
- Personal bell notifications live in `user_notifications`, written only by DB triggers/functions (task-comment trigger, daily due digest via cron plus `project_due_digest_me` on app open, deduped per user/day) — why: per-user, idempotent and works even without the scheduler.
- Task conversations reuse `LeadNotesChat` through its `controller` prop; tasks with a time are timed Agenda events (blocked by calendar_events_no_overlap), tasks without time stay all-day — why: one chat UI and one per-person conflict rule.
- Deleting a project removes its storage files first (`deleteProjectCompletely`), then the row — why: DB cascade does not delete storage objects.
